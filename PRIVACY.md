# deleks privacy policy

*Last updated November 21, 2026.*

deleks collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking,
and it makes no network connections.

The game keeps three things on your device, in the app's own settings: your best score,
whether safe-move hints are on, and which look you chose. They never leave the device, and
deleting the app deletes them.

The About box links to the source code on GitHub and to origamisoftware.com. Those links
open in your browser, and those sites' own privacy policies apply there.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/smarks/deleks/issues.

deleks is made by Spencer Marks, Origami Software.
